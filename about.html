Our story

News that feels like
a friend.

You know that one friend who reads everything? The one who shows up at chai time and tells you exactly what happened — funny, sharp, no jargon? The Dawn Brief is that friend.

Built for Indian students and professionals who do not have time for newspapers but cannot afford to be uninformed. We write news the way it should be told — like a message from someone who actually gets your life.

"I built this because I was that student who felt left out in GD rounds. Not because I was not smart. But because nobody had told me what happened in the world in a way I could actually understand and remember."

How it works

Every morning at 6:00 AM IST, The Dawn Brief fetches the most important stories from across India and the world. Our AI reads them, understands them, and rewrites each one in your voice.

If you are a student — it sounds like your funniest, sharpest batchmate explaining things between lectures. If you are a professional — it sounds like that one colleague at the coffee machine who always knows what is going on.

Same news. Different experience. Every single morning.
